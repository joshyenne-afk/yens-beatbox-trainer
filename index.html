Practice Modes
Record

Four-count, then the app goes completely silent and records you. Play one clean pass — that's the looper condition. Hear it back against a freshly generated click, so drift shows up as your take walking away from a grid that never moved. Tap to cycle 1 / 2 / 4 bars.

Tempo

Ten clean loops, then +2 BPM with a fresh count-off, repeating until you hit the groove's target tempo.

Time

Call and response. The app plays two bars, then hands you two bars with only a click. Your job is to come back in on the downbeat.

Loop one half of the pattern at a time. Useful when one specific move is the thing breaking — drill that half until it isn't.

Build-up

Mute voices to layer the groove up instead of swallowing it whole: kick alone until it's solid, then kick + snare, then add the hat. It's the same logic as learning a drum part limb by limb — each layer has to be automatic before the next one goes on top, or the new one drags the old one out of time.
